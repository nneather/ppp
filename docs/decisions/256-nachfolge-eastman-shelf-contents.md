## Built

- Brunnen *Nachfolge* (ISBN 9783765509483, copyright page 2016, Zimmerling). Thirteen sections, same passages as the English *Cost of Discipleship*. Die Bergpredigt is one row (Matthew 5–7, pp. 104–198), not the beatitude subsections. Die Boten follows the contents header Matthew 9:35–10:42, split at the German subsection pages. Verse bounds for the call, the cross, the individual, and the six messenger parts are the same chapters as the English rows.
- Susan Eastman, *Paul and the Person*. Three sections from the shelf contents: Romans 7, pp. 109–125; Philippians 2, pp. 126–150; Galatians 2, pp. 151–175. Chapter-only ranges (the contents name the chapter, not a verse slice).

## Decided

- Part I “Die teure Gnade” and all of Part II (baptism, the body of Christ, the visible community, the saints, the image of Christ) stay out. They are not one passage.
- Eastman chapters 1–3 and the conclusion stay out (Epictetus, contemporary accounts of the person, participation as a theme, a closing sketch).
- Eastman `year` and `isbn` stay null. The contents match the Eerdmans edition; the copyright page was not in the photos.
